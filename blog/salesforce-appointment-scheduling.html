Salesforce Appointment Scheduling: Set Times for Every Advisor, No Code

Offer the exact appointment times you choose, give each advisor their own, and let staff or clients book them, inside Salesforce or on your Experience Cloud site.

Published November 12, 2026 · MultiDatePick

How do I schedule appointments in Salesforce without code?

Add the MultiDatePick Resource Booking calendar to a record page, an app page, a Screen Flow or an Experience Cloud site, and point it at your own appointment object and your advisors. Then choose the start times you offer, the appointment length, minimum notice, limits per client and closed days in the Setup Wizard. Staff book inside Salesforce, clients book on your site, and every appointment is a record on your own object.

"How do clients book an appointment with us in Salesforce?" "Can each advisor set their own appointment times?" "How do I stop people booking at 9:17?" "Can a client see who else is booked?" An appointment is a simple thing until a real business runs on it, and then every one of those questions needs an answer.

MultiDatePick answers them on the objects you already have. Pick an advisor, see only the start times that advisor offers, pick one, and book. The appointment saves as a record on your own object, with its status, its client and its length, and no other client ever sees a name. No code, no Apex, no add-on license.

What appointment scheduling needs, and what you get

Only these times

List the start times you offer, such as 9:00, 10:30, 1:00, 2:30 and 4:00. The grid shows those and nothing else.

Each advisor's own times

One text field on the advisor holds their own list. Pick the advisor and the grid follows it.

The right length

A 30, 45 or 60 minute appointment books in one click, with a buffer after each one if you want it.

Busy only

Other clients' appointments show as Busy, with no name, no details and no link, enforced on the server.

Rules that protect the day

Minimum notice, how far ahead, a limit per client and closed days, checked again at every save.

After the booking

Statuses from Requested to Confirmed, a Flow button for reminders, and Add to Calendar for the client.

Offer only the appointment times you choose

Most calendars cut the day into equal slots: every 15 or 30 minutes from opening to close. That suits a meeting room. It does not suit a practice that sees clients at 9:00, 10:30, 1:00, 2:30 and 4:00, with the gaps between kept for notes, calls and lunch.

Only these times fixes that. In the Setup Wizard, choose Only these times and add each start time. The time grid then offers those starts and no others, on every day and in every view. Each one lasts the Booking Length, so a 60 minute length turns 10:30 into a 10:30 to 11:30 appointment in one click.

Everything else still guards a listed time. A time that is already booked, closed for the day, too soon for your notice period, outside the advisor's hours or too close to the next appointment shows greyed out, so clients see the time exists and is taken, instead of a gap they cannot explain.

Each advisor, their own times and hours

One advisor works mornings, another sees clients at 8:00 before the office opens, a third keeps Fridays for reviews. Add a text field to your advisor object, such as Available Times, and type each advisor's starts in 24-hour form: 08:00,09:30,11:00. Point Only These Times Field at it (in the Setup Wizard, Each Resource's Own Times), and when a client picks that advisor, the grid shows that advisor's times. An advisor with the field left blank uses the calendar's own list.

Working hours come from the advisor too: Business Hours Start Field and Business Hours End Field read two time fields on the advisor record. A day off, a training afternoon or an office closure goes in your closures object with an optional advisor, so it closes the day for one advisor or for everyone, and can show its reason on the calendar. When one appointment needs two people, such as an advisor and a specialist, only the times both of them offer are shown.

Booked by staff in Salesforce, or by clients on your site

The same calendar works in both places. Staff book from a record page, an app page or a Screen Flow, with every detail visible: who is booked, for what, and in what status. Clients book on a public Experience Cloud page without logging in. The Record details pop-up asks them for their name, email, the meeting type and a note before the appointment saves.

On that public page, other clients' appointments show as Busy. Set Show Other People's Bookings As to Busy for site guests, and staff still see every name while visitors see a hatched Unavailable block with no name, no hover card and no link. The names are removed on the server, so they never reach the visitor's browser. See how to build a public booking page on Experience Cloud.

Rules that keep the appointment book sane

Each rule is one setting, and each is checked again on the server when the appointment saves, so reloading the page never gets around it.

The ruleWhat it does for appointments
Minimum noticeMinimum Notice (Hours): 24 requires a full day of notice. Earlier days are greyed out, and on the cut-off day the earlier times close.
How far aheadBook Up To (Days Ahead): 60 keeps the book open two months out, and moves forward every day.
Limit per clientLimit per Person: how many upcoming appointments one client can hold, counted by their email or contact in Limit per Person Client Field. Staff can book past it after a confirmation; site visitors cannot.
Per dayMax Bookings per Day: how many separate appointments one person can pick on a single day.
Closed daysHolidays, office closures and days off, for the whole office or one advisor, for a whole day or part of one, with the reason shown.
Breathing roomBuffer Between Bookings (Minutes): 15 keeps a quarter hour free after each appointment.
No double bookingA taken time is greyed out before anyone clicks, and the save checks again. How conflicts and capacity work.

Confirmations and statuses

An appointment moves through a life: requested, confirmed, met, sometimes cancelled or missed. Point Status Field at your status picklist and each status gets its own color. Selectable Statuses decides what a booking can be saved as: set it to Requested on the public page, and every client request arrives as Requested for your team to confirm. Add Cancelled to Ignored Statuses, and a cancelled appointment frees its time at once.

The client gets a confirmation the moment they book: turn on Show In-Component Toast and write your own words in Success Toast Message, such as "Thank you. We will confirm your appointment within one business day."

Reminders and follow-ups with a Flow button

The Setup Wizard's custom buttons run one of your own Flows. Put a Send a reminder button on every appointment, and staff send the reminder from the calendar in one click. A button can show only on certain statuses, such as Confirmed, act on several appointments picked in Edit Mode, or run on its own after every save, so a Flow can send the confirmation email without anyone pressing anything.

Add to Calendar for the client

Turn on Show Add to Calendar After Saving, and the client leaves with the appointment in Outlook, Google or Apple Calendar: the right date and time, the advisor's name in the title. It is a calendar file made in the browser, so public site guests get it too, and nothing leaves Salesforce until they download it. More on Add to Calendar.

Questions people ask

How do I schedule appointments in Salesforce without code?

Add the MultiDatePick Resource Booking calendar to a record page, an app page, a Screen Flow or an Experience Cloud site, and point it at your own appointment object and your advisors. Then choose the start times you offer, the appointment length, minimum notice, limits per client and closed days in the Setup Wizard. Staff book inside Salesforce, clients book on your site, and every appointment is a record on your own object.

Can I offer only set appointment times, like 9:00 and 10:30?

Yes. Choose Only these times in the Setup Wizard and add each start time, for example 9:00, 10:30, 1:00, 2:30 and 4:00. The time grid shows only those starts, each lasting the Booking Length. A listed time that is taken, closed or too soon shows greyed out.

Can each advisor have their own appointment times?

Yes. Add a text field to your advisor object, type each advisor's start times in 24-hour form, such as 08:00,09:30,11:00, and set Only These Times Field to it. When a client picks that advisor, the grid shows that advisor's times. Each advisor's working hours can come from two time fields on their record too.

Can clients book appointments on an Experience Cloud site without logging in?

Yes. The calendar works on public Experience Cloud pages for guests. A pop-up asks for their name, email and other details before the appointment saves, and every limit, notice period and conflict check runs on the server.

Can a client see other clients' appointments?

Not when you choose Busy. Set Show Other People's Bookings As to Busy for site guests, or Busy for everyone, and other appointments draw as a hatched Unavailable block with no name, no details and no link. The names are removed on the server, so they never reach the browser.

Do I need Salesforce Scheduler to book appointments in Salesforce?

No. Salesforce Scheduler is an add-on license with its own data model. MultiDatePick books appointments on the objects you already have, custom or standard, for $20 per user per month with every feature included. See how the two compare.
