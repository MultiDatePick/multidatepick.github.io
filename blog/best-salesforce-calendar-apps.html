The Best Salesforce Calendar Apps and LWC Calendar Components (2026)

Nine ways to put a calendar in Salesforce, from the built-in Calendar to code your developers maintain. For picking dates, booking time and saving records with no code, MultiDatePick is the one to beat.

Published November 10, 2026 · MultiDatePick

What is the best calendar app for Salesforce?

For scheduling on your own objects, MultiDatePick: a no-code Salesforce calendar LWC with multi-date picking, time slots, resource booking with capacity, six views and guest booking on Experience Cloud, all set up in a wizard. The built-in Calendar suits viewing Events, and Salesforce Scheduler suits appointment matching on its own data model.

“What is the best calendar app for Salesforce?” “Is there a customizable LWC calendar?” “Can customers book without logging in?” The honest answer depends on the job: looking at Events, matching customers to staff, or letting people pick dates and book time on your own objects. This guide sorts the options by that job.

We make MultiDatePick, so read this with that in mind. Every statement about another product comes from its own public pages as of November 2026, and where we could not confirm a detail, we left it out.

How to choose

  • Booking rooms, equipment or people with a seat limit: the Salesforce booking calendar.
  • Customers booking on your website: guest booking on Experience Cloud.
  • Weighing Salesforce’s own appointment product: Lightning Scheduler and an alternative.
  • Picking many dates on a record or in a flow: the multi-date picker LWC.
  • Time slots at any interval: the Salesforce time slot picker.
  • Classes and courses with seats: Salesforce class scheduling.
  • Comparing a code-first option: FullCalendar in Salesforce vs MultiDatePick.

What we left out, and why

Scheduling-link tools that book meetings outside Salesforce and sync them back, and vertical booking suites that run a whole industry’s operations, are different kinds of product from a calendar you put on a Salesforce page. If a link in an email is all you need, one of those may be enough. If the booking belongs on your records, with your rules, you need a calendar inside Salesforce.

Questions people ask

What is the best calendar app for Salesforce?

For scheduling on your own objects, MultiDatePick: a no-code Salesforce calendar LWC with multi-date picking, time slots, resource booking with capacity, six views and guest booking on Experience Cloud, all set up in a wizard. The built-in Calendar suits viewing Events, and Salesforce Scheduler suits appointment matching on its own data model.

Is there a customizable LWC calendar for Salesforce?

Yes. MultiDatePick is a Lightning Web Component calendar with 130+ settings set in a wizard: objects and fields, how people pick dates and times, six views, status colors, header branding and buttons that run your own Flow. It goes on record, App and Home pages, Screen Flows and Experience Cloud sites.

Does Salesforce have a built-in booking calendar?

Not for your own objects. The standard Calendar shows Events and records but does not take bookings with capacity, and Salesforce Scheduler books appointments on its own data model with an add-on license. MultiDatePick adds a booking calendar to any object.

Can I use FullCalendar in Salesforce?

Yes, wrapped in an LWC that your developers build and maintain: the data layer, the save, the booking rules and the security are your code, and its timeline and resource views need a Premium license. MultiDatePick gives you those views as settings, with no code.

Which Salesforce calendar lets Experience Cloud guests book without logging in?

MultiDatePick Resource Booking works on public Experience Cloud pages: guests pick a resource, a day and an open slot, with capacity and conflict checks before Save. Salesforce Scheduler also has a guest booking flow, built on its own data model.
