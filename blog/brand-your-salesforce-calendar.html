Brand Your Salesforce Calendar: Your Logo, Colors and a Banner Photo

Put your logo, your name and your brand colors on the calendar's top band, add a banner photo, and watch a live preview change as you go. No code, no CSS.

Published November 9, 2026 · Updated November 10, 2026 · MultiDatePick

Can I put my logo and brand colors on a Salesforce calendar or booking page?

Yes. In the MultiDatePick Setup Wizard, add your logo, your company name, a line under the title and a note, pick a band color and a button color, and add a banner photo. The header shows on every view, on record and App pages, in Screen Flows and on public Experience Cloud booking pages that guests use. No code.

"How do I add our logo to a Salesforce calendar?" "Can the booking page match our brand colors?" "How do I make an Experience Cloud booking page look like our website?" A calendar component dropped on a page arrives in its maker's colors, and changing them usually takes a stylesheet, a static resource or a developer.

MultiDatePick gives the calendar's top band your brand: your logo, your name and a line or two of your own, your band and button colors, and a banner photo in four looks. It all lives in one box in the Setup Wizard, with a live preview at the top. No code, no CSS, no static resource.

What you get

Your logo

Upload it, pick one of your images in Salesforce Files, or paste an image link. It sits left of the title, or above it, centered.

Your name and words

Your company name above the calendar's title, a line under it, and a note such as your opening hours.

Your brand colors

A band color and a button color: six and five ready swatches, or any hex code from your brand guide.

Always readable

Automatic picks dark or light words for your band, and deepens your color under white words until it passes 4.5:1 contrast.

A banner photo

Behind the band, as a cover strip, under dark panels, or tinted in your band color. Shade, focus and height are yours.

Ready for guests

An uploaded logo or photo gets a public link, so visitors on a public Experience Cloud site see it too.

Your logo, your name, your words

The calendar keeps its own title. Around it you can add three lines: a Name above the title for your company or team (up to 80 characters), a Line under the title, and a Note (up to 160 characters each) for what the page is for and when you are open.

The logo shows up to 44 px high and 180 px wide (64 px high on the Cover strip), on a small white tile so it reads on any band color. A logo made for dark backgrounds can sit straight on the band: untick White tile behind the logo. Put it left of the title, or above, centered for a page that feels like a landing page.

Brand colors that stay readable

Pick a Band color from six swatches, or type your brand's hex code. Pick a Button color from five, or your own. The button color paints the Today button, the outline on today's date, and the days and time slots people pick. A dark or mid-tone band color carries into the calendar's own buttons, such as Save or Request Appointment, and into the selected view. A light band keeps the standard navy there, so the buttons stay crisp.

Words on the band looks after contrast. On Automatic, MultiDatePick writes white words on a dark band and dark words on a light one, whichever reads better. Where your color sits under white words, on a button or the selected view, it is deepened just enough to pass 4.5:1 contrast, the WCAG AA level for text. Prefer to choose? Set Light or Dark yourself: the wizard flags a choice that is hard to read on your color, and one that cannot be read falls back to Automatic.

A banner photo, four ways

Turn on Banner image and add a photo of your office, your building or your city. Then pick the look:

  • Behind the band: the photo fills the band and the words sit on it.
  • Cover strip: a strip of photo across the top, your band color under it, and the logo on its edge, like a profile page.
  • Controls on a panel: the photo shows in full, with the name and the calendar's controls on dark panels.
  • Tinted in band color: the photo washed in your band color, for a calm look in your own color.

Shade darkens the photo so the words stay readable, from 0 to 90 percent, and the wizard warns you when a light shade could make the words hard to read on a bright photo. On the tinted look, it sets how strongly your color covers the photo. Height makes the band Compact, Standard or Tall.

Your brand on a public booking page

Branding matters most where customers see it. On a public Experience Cloud site the calendar is the page, so it should look like your company, not like a plug-in.

An image you upload in the wizard, or pick from your Salesforce Files, gets a public link. Guests who never log in see your logo and photo too, on every view the page offers.

Setting up the page itself takes five point-and-click steps. See how to build a public booking page on Experience Cloud.

Made for narrow screens

On a phone the band narrows and the photo is cropped to fit. Keep in view chooses the part that stays on screen: center, top, bottom, left or right. The skyline, the storefront or the campus never slides out of frame.

Set it up in the Setup Wizard

No code, no CSS and no static resource. Four steps:

  1. Open Other settings. In the MultiDatePick Setup Wizard, open the Other settings tab of your config and find the Header box, with its live preview at the top.
  2. Add the logo and words. Upload a logo, pick one of your images in Salesforce Files or paste a link. Then type the Name, the Line under the title and the Note.
  3. Pick the colors. Click a band and a button swatch, or type your hex codes. Leave Words on the band on Automatic.
  4. Add a photo, then save. Turn on Banner image, choose the photo and a look, check the header once more on the Review step, and save the config. Every page that uses its Config Name shows the new header on its next load.

Nothing changes for anyone until an admin sets it: a calendar with no Header keeps its standard band.

Prefer App Builder? The same settings travel together as one property, Header, on all three components: Dates, Dates & Times and Resource Booking. It works on record, app and Home pages, on Experience Cloud pages and in Flow screens.

Who brands their calendar

Anyone whose calendar is something customers, members or staff look at every day:

Financial advisorsA client booking page in the firm's colors, with the logo clients already trust.
Clinics and practicesAppointment pages that carry the clinic's name, with its opening hours in the note.
UniversitiesAdvising and tutoring appointments in school colors, with a campus photo behind the band.
Multi-location brandsOne config per location, each with its own photo and name, all in the same brand colors.
NonprofitsVolunteer sign-ups under the cause's own logo and a photo from the field.
Company appsA Home page calendar in company colors, so it looks like part of your app, not an add-on.

Each one is a control in the Setup Wizard's Header box. In App Builder, Experience Builder and Flow Builder they travel together as JSON in one property, Header (headerStyle), which the wizard writes for you, for example {"logo":"https://...","name":"ACME Financial","band":"#14532D","button":"#FBBF24"}.

Questions people ask

Can I put my logo and brand colors on a Salesforce calendar or booking page?

Yes. In the MultiDatePick Setup Wizard, add your logo, your company name, a line under the title and a note, pick a band color and a button color, and add a banner photo. The header shows on every view, on record and App pages, in Screen Flows and on public Experience Cloud booking pages that guests use. No code.

How do I add a logo to a Salesforce calendar?

Open the MultiDatePick Setup Wizard, go to Other settings and use the Header box: upload your logo, pick one of your images in Salesforce Files, or paste an image link. It shows up to 44 px high, left of the calendar's title or above it, centered. Save the config, and every page that uses it shows the logo. No code and no CSS.

Can a Salesforce booking calendar match our brand colors?

Yes. Pick a band color and a button color from the swatches, or type your brand's hex codes. With Words on the band set to Automatic, MultiDatePick writes dark or light words, whichever reads better on your color, and deepens your color under white words until it passes 4.5:1 contrast.

Do guests on an Experience Cloud site see the logo and banner photo?

Yes. An image you upload in the wizard, or pick from your Salesforce Files, gets a public link, so visitors who never log in see it too. It is a standard Salesforce public link, so Content Deliveries and Public Links need to be on in Setup.

Can I use an image link from another website?

Yes. Paste any https image link. Salesforce shows pictures from another website once that site is added in Setup under Trusted URLs, so add it there, or upload the image instead and skip that step. If a link ever fails to load, the calendar shows your band without the picture, and the wizard tells you under the link.

Does the header work in a Screen Flow or on an app page?

Yes. Header is a setting on all three components, Dates, Dates & Times and Resource Booking, on record, app and Home pages, Experience Cloud pages and Flow screens. Set it in the Setup Wizard and use the Config Name, or paste the JSON into the Header property in App Builder, Experience Builder or Flow Builder.
