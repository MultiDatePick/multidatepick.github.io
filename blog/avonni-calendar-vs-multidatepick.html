Avonni Calendar vs MultiDatePick: A Component Library or a Booking Calendar?

Avonni offers a wide library of Salesforce components, a calendar and scheduler among them. MultiDatePick is one calendar suite built around picking dates and booking time. How they compare, and when to pick each.

Published November 2, 2026 · MultiDatePick

Avonni is a familiar name for Salesforce admins who build with components: data tables, Kanban boards, charts, and a Calendar that doubles as a Scheduler. It is a toolkit, and its calendar is one piece of it. MultiDatePick is narrower and deeper: a calendar suite whose whole job is dates, time slots and bookings saved as Salesforce records.

Both are native and configured without code. Which one fits depends on whether you need a broad toolkit or booking rules out of the box.

Where Avonni is the better choice

  • You want one vendor for many kinds of components, not just a calendar.
  • You need a vertical timeline or ICS feeds shown next to your Salesforce events.
  • Your calendar mostly displays records, and the scheduling logic already lives elsewhere.
  • Your team already builds pages with Avonni and knows its builder.

Where MultiDatePick is the better choice

Booking rules built in

Capacity, conflicts, notice, booking window, buffers and closures, with nothing to wire up.

Six record shapes

A toolkit or a finished feature

A component library gives you building blocks: you decide what the calendar shows, then wire up what happens when someone drags or clicks. That is ideal when your process is unusual and you have the time to shape it. MultiDatePick ships the finished scheduling behavior: pick, check, save. If what you need is "let people book rooms without double booking" or "let a manager pick the twelve dates of a training series", it is a configuration, not a build.

Questions people ask

Is MultiDatePick an Avonni Calendar alternative?

For booking with capacity and conflict checks, multi-date picking and public booking pages, yes. If you want a whole library of components from one vendor, Avonni covers more ground beyond the calendar.

Does MultiDatePick need Apex?

No. It is set up in the Setup Wizard or in Lightning App Builder, on any standard or custom object, and every query and save runs as the viewing user.

Can MultiDatePick show a resource timeline?

Yes. The timeline lists rooms, people or equipment down the side and days across, with drag to move or resize, and it is included with every license.

Can I use MultiDatePick inside a Screen Flow?

Yes. Add the component to a screen and set Config Name. Picked dates, ranges and times come out as Flow variables, or the calendar saves the records itself.
