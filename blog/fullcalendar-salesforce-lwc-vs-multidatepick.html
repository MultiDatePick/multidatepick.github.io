FullCalendar in Salesforce vs MultiDatePick: Which LWC Calendar Is More Customizable?

One is a JavaScript engine your developers have to build a calendar on. The other is a finished Salesforce calendar you configure in an afternoon. For almost every Salesforce team, MultiDatePick is the better choice. Here is the checklist, item by item.

Published November 1, 2026 · MultiDatePick

Ask for "a super customizable LWC calendar for Salesforce" and two names come up again and again: FullCalendar, the open-source JavaScript calendar that now ships an official Lightning Web Component wrapper, and MultiDatePick, a native calendar and booking suite. Both are called customizable. Only one of them works on your Salesforce records the day you install it.

FullCalendar customizes through code: you pass an options object, write callbacks, and build the Salesforce side yourself (the Apex that loads records, the save, the permissions). MultiDatePick customizes through settings: more than 130 of them across its three components, set in a Setup Wizard or in Lightning App Builder, on any standard or custom object. No Apex required.

The short answer: choose MultiDatePick if you want a calendar running on your Salesforce data now, with booking rules, security and translations already done and no Premium license to buy. FullCalendar only makes sense if you are building a calendar product of your own and have developers to own it for years.

When FullCalendar still makes sense

In a narrow case: you are building a calendar product from scratch, you need a view type no calendar ships, or your events live outside Salesforce, and you have developers to build and maintain it. For everything else, choosing FullCalendar means rebuilding MultiDatePick yourself: the Apex that loads records, the save, capacity and conflict checks, permissions, tests and translations, before your users see a single booking.

Not just for bookings

MultiDatePick is known for booking, but it is also a calendar for records you already have. Put it on an Account page and show the account's tasks, including those on its contacts and opportunities. Questions people ask

Is FullCalendar free in Salesforce?

The core library is open source. The timeline and resource views are Premium features that need a commercial license, and the Salesforce side (loading records, saving, security and tests) is code you write and maintain.

Does MultiDatePick need Apex or a developer?

No. You set it up in the Setup Wizard or in Lightning App Builder, on any standard or custom object. Records load and save as the viewing user, with sharing and field-level security applied.

Can MultiDatePick show Tasks, Events and Opportunities?

Yes. Any object with a Date or Date/Time field works, standard or custom. A Rollup Field adds records that point to the page record from further away, such as tasks on an account's contacts.

Does MultiDatePick include a resource timeline?

Yes. Timeline, week, utilization heatmap and agenda views are included with the calendar, with no extra license. One plan has every feature.
