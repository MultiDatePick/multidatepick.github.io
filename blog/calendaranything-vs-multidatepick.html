CalendarAnything vs MultiDatePick: Which Salesforce Calendar Fits Your Team?

CalendarAnything is a long-established app for viewing Salesforce records on calendars. MultiDatePick is a calendar for picking dates, booking time and saving records. Here is where each one is stronger.

Published November 2, 2026 · MultiDatePick

If you search for a Salesforce calendar app, CalendarAnything comes up early. It has been on the AppExchange for years, and it is good at what it was built for: putting many kinds of Salesforce records on shared calendars that teams browse, filter and print. MultiDatePick starts from the other end: the moment someone picks dates or books a time, and the records that come out of it.

Both are native, both work with standard and custom objects, and both are configured without code. The difference is the job. Read on for a side-by-side, and an honest list of where CalendarAnything is the better pick.

Where CalendarAnything is the better choice

  • You want one place to browse many calendars from many objects, layered and filtered, like a shared team calendar.
  • You need Gantt, swimlane or Kanban layouts.
  • Syncing with Google or Outlook calendars matters more than booking rules.
  • You print calendars or need the mobile app's offline mode.
  • You want a vendor with many years of installs behind the product.

Where MultiDatePick is the better choice

Many dates, one action

Click scattered days, drag ranges or add a recurring pattern, and save them as child records in one go.

Booking that holds

Live capacity badges, conflict checks on save and several resources in one booking.

Rules on every pick

Notice, booking window, buffers, business hours and closures, enforced on pick and on drag.

Inside Screen Flows

Picks become Flow variables, so a flow can route, approve or save them its own way.

Public booking

Guests book on an Experience Cloud site with the same rules as your staff.

Six record shapes

One record per date, per range, per time block, per resource, or a parent with sub-blocks.

Can you use both?

Yes. Many teams use a viewing calendar for the big picture and a booking calendar where people actually schedule. MultiDatePick saves ordinary Salesforce records on your own objects, so anything that reads those objects, CalendarAnything included, shows what was booked.

See the booking side live. The live demo is a public booking site with advisors, capacity and blocked time.

Questions people ask

Is MultiDatePick a CalendarAnything alternative?

For booking, multi-date picking and public scheduling pages, yes. For browsing many calendars from many objects with Gantt or Kanban layouts and Google or Outlook sync, CalendarAnything covers more. Some teams use both.

How much does CalendarAnything cost compared with MultiDatePick?

Silverline's FAQ lists CalendarAnything at $199 per user per year with a minimum of 10 licenses. MultiDatePick is $20 per user per month with every feature included and a 30-day free trial.

Does MultiDatePick sync with Outlook or Google Calendar?

It offers an Add to Calendar file after each save, which opens in Outlook, Google Calendar and Apple Calendar. It does not keep a two-way sync.

Can MultiDatePick show records from any object?

Yes. Any standard or custom object with a Date or Date/Time field, including Task and Event, and records reached through up to five lookups with the Rollup Field.
