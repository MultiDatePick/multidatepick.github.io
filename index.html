Frequently asked questions

Short answers. More on the FAQ page.

Do I need to write any code?

No. You configure everything in the Setup Wizard, Lightning App Builder or Flow Builder. To process date ranges in a Flow, drag the included Parse Date Entries from JSON action into the flow. No Apex required.

What is capacity mode?

Capacity mode lets several bookings share a time slot up to a limit you set with a number field on the resource, such as 10 seats. Each slot shows a live badge like 3/10 and stays open until it is full. With a status field set, each slot also shows the mix of statuses across its bookings.

Can users select recurring dates?

Yes. Every component has a Recurring Pattern option: weekdays, weekends or any days of the week, every 1, 2, 3 or N weeks, and monthly occurrences such as the 1st Monday, the 2nd and 4th Friday or the last Saturday. A 50-click task becomes two clicks.

Does my data leave Salesforce?

No. MultiDatePick is 100% native and runs entirely inside your org, with no external servers and no callouts. It has passed the Salesforce AppExchange Security Review. See Trust & Security.

What Salesforce editions are supported?

Any edition that supports Lightning Web Components and Screen Flows: Enterprise, Unlimited, Performance, Developer and Salesforce Platform, plus industry clouds such as Nonprofit, Education, Health, Financial Services, Public Sector and Manufacturing. Essentials and Professional are not supported because Salesforce restricts custom Lightning components there.

How much does MultiDatePick cost?

$20 per user per month. One plan with every feature, including resource booking and capacity, and a 30-day free trial with no card required. See pricing.
