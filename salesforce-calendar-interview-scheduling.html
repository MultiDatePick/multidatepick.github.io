Salesforce interview scheduling questions

Short answers to what people search.

Can you schedule interviews in Salesforce?

Yes, with MultiDatePick. Recruiters pick one or many dates and the time slots on one screen, and each interview saves as a record on your own object, linked to the candidate. Existing interviews are drawn on the grid, so taken slots are visible before anyone books.

Can interview slots be 45 minutes?

Yes. Time Interval (Minutes) takes any number. 15, 30 or 60 minutes are typical, and 45 or 90 work the same way.

How do I stop an interviewer being double booked?

Make the interviewer the resource, often the User object, and set Conflict Behavior to Block. Every save is checked against existing bookings. For a panel, add a Capacity Field so a slot stays open until every seat is filled.

Can candidates book their own interview?

Yes. Put the same component on an Experience Cloud site page and set Config Name and Static Parent Record Id. Candidates see only open slots inside interviewer hours, and the booking saves straight into your org.

How is this different from Salesforce Scheduler?

Salesforce Scheduler is a paid add-on built on its own data model for appointments. MultiDatePick books on your own objects, with multi-date selection, capacity and five calendar views. See the full comparison of Salesforce Lightning Scheduler and MultiDatePick.

How much does MultiDatePick cost?

$20 per user per month for every component and feature, with a 30-day free trial and no card required. It is 100% Salesforce native and coming soon to the Salesforce AppExchange.
