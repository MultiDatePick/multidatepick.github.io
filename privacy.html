Privacy Policy

What multidatepick.com collects, why, and how to change your choice. Analytics and source tracking run only with your OK, and nothing is sold or used for ads.

Last updated: November 12, 2026

This policy covers this website. The MultiDatePick app itself runs inside your Salesforce org and sends nothing to us: see Trust & Security for how the app handles your data.

At a glance

  • Analytics: Google Analytics, with cookies only after you accept.
  • Where you came from: saved in your browser only after you accept.
  • Forms: what you type goes to MultiDatePick's Salesforce org, to reply to you.
  • Advertising: none. No ad cookies, and nothing is sold.

Your cookie choice

On your first visit, a bar at the bottom of the page asks before anything that tracks you is stored. Accept all, Reject all and Settings are always one click away, and rejecting is as easy as accepting.

Your choice is saved in your browser's local storage (under the name mdp_consent) with the date you made it. We ask again after 12 months, or sooner if the choices on offer change. Clearing this site's data in your browser also clears it.

Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 to see which pages people read and how they find the site, so we can improve it. It records the pages you view, the page that linked you here, your device type, browser, screen size, language and approximate location (country or city, worked out from your IP address). Google says Google Analytics 4 does not store IP addresses. When you send one of our forms, it also counts that a form was sent, without anything you typed.

If you accept Analytics, Google Analytics sets two cookies, _ga and _ga_YE5L0ECBSY, that tell a returning visit from a new one. By default they last up to 2 years.

If you reject it, or have not chosen yet, the site uses Google Consent Mode: no Google Analytics cookies are set, and each page sends Google a basic signal without cookies or any ID that follows you between visits. Google may use these signals to estimate overall visit counts.

Advertising signals are always off. Every page tells Google that ad storage, ad user data and ad personalization are denied, whatever you choose.

How long Google keeps it: detailed visit data is deleted after the retention period set in the Google Analytics account. Google's default is 2 months, and the longest it allows is 14 months. Report totals are kept. Google's own policy explains how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Where you came from (first-touch tracking)

If you accept Where you came from, the site saves one small record in your browser's local storage (under the name mdp_first_touch): the campaign tags in the link you followed (utm_source, utm_medium, utm_campaign, utm_term, utm_content), the page that linked you here, the first page you opened and the date.

It stays on your device and is not sent anywhere on its own. It goes to us only if you send one of our forms. If you switch the setting off or reject all, the record is deleted from your browser. Without it, the forms work exactly the same and send no source details.

The Notify Me and Custom Config forms

The Notify Me at Launch and Free Custom Config forms send what you type (your name, work email, company and, for a custom config, your answers about the calendar you need) to MultiDatePick's own Salesforce org, through Salesforce's standard web form service. If you accepted Where you came from, the details above go with it.

We use it to reply to you and, if you asked to be notified, to send launch news about MultiDatePick. We do not sell it or share it for anyone else's marketing. To have your details changed or deleted, email support@multidatepick.com.

Other things the site remembers

If you close the announcement bar at the top of the page, the site remembers that until you close the browser tab (session storage). It is not sent anywhere.

Who hosts the site and the Test Drive

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages, run by GitHub. GitHub handles the requests your browser makes and may log technical details such as IP addresses to run and protect its service. See the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

The live Test Drive is a Salesforce Experience Cloud site, hosted by Salesforce, with its own cookies and policies. See the Salesforce Privacy Statement.

Links to X, LinkedIn and YouTube are plain links: nothing loads from those sites until you click one.

Questions and contact

For any question about this policy or your data, email support@multidatepick.com. If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page changes with it.
