# MultiDatePick: Salesforce Calendar and Multi-Date Picker

**[multidatepick.com](https://multidatepick.com/)** · [Getting Started](https://multidatepick.com/gettingstarted.html) · [Config Library](https://multidatepick.com/configlibrary.html) · [Blog](https://multidatepick.com/blog/) · [Pricing](https://multidatepick.com/pricing.html)

MultiDatePick is a Salesforce calendar for many dates, times and bookings. Standard Salesforce stores one date or one start and end range per record; MultiDatePick lets users click as many days as they need, add times, and book rooms, people or equipment, and it saves every pick as a record on your own objects. No Apex required.

[![Salesforce booking calendar with appointment statuses in color](https://multidatepick.com/images/salesforce-booking-calendar-status-colors.png)](https://multidatepick.com/)

## Three components, one package

| Component | What it does | Guide |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Date** | Many dates, ranges and recurring patterns, with status colors | [Salesforce multi-date picker](https://multidatepick.com/multidatepickdates.html) |
| **Date Time** | Dates plus a time slot grid and conflict checks | [Salesforce date and time slot picker](https://multidatepick.com/multidatepickdatetime.html) |
| **Booking** | Rooms, equipment or people, with availability and capacity | [Salesforce booking calendar](https://multidatepick.com/salesforce-booking-calendar.html) |

## Five views of the same records

Calendar, [Timeline](https://multidatepick.com/blog/salesforce-timeline-view.html), Week, [Utilization](https://multidatepick.com/blog/salesforce-utilization-heatmap.html) and Agenda. See [all five views](https://multidatepick.com/blog/salesforce-calendar-views.html).

## Where it runs
