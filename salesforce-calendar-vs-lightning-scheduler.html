What is Salesforce Lightning Scheduler?

Salesforce's own appointment scheduling product, now called Salesforce Scheduler. It is strong at one job: matching a customer to the right qualified person, at the right branch, at the right time.

Built forCustomer appointments, such as banks and clinics booking clients with qualified staff at a branch.
BookingGuided Inbound (customer books) and Outbound (staff books) flow templates, plus a guest flow for prospects.
ExtrasConcurrent scheduling, multi-resource appointments (up to five required resources), attendee limits, maps and location search.
LicenseAn extra-cost add-on in Lightning Experience on Enterprise, Performance and Unlimited editions. Each service resource gets the Salesforce Scheduler permission set license.

Salesforce Lightning Scheduler questions

Short answers to what people search.

What is Salesforce Lightning Scheduler?

Salesforce's own appointment scheduling product. It matches a customer to a qualified person at a location and time, using service territories, work type groups, work types, service resources and service appointments. Guided Inbound and Outbound flows do the booking.

Is Lightning Scheduler the same as Salesforce Scheduler?

Yes. Lightning Scheduler is the earlier name. Salesforce documentation and Trailhead now call it Salesforce Scheduler, and some license names still say Lightning Scheduler.

Does Salesforce Scheduler cost extra?

Yes. It is available for an extra cost in Lightning Experience on Enterprise, Performance and Unlimited editions, and each service resource needs the Salesforce Scheduler permission set license. Asset Scheduling for rooms and equipment is a further extra cost. MultiDatePick is one plan at $20 per user per month with a 30-day free trial.

Does Salesforce Scheduler work on Experience Cloud?

Yes. The Inbound New Guest Appointment flow goes on a public Experience Cloud page, after you give guest users access to service resources, service territories, work types and work type groups. MultiDatePick also runs on Experience Cloud pages, reading and saving your own objects, and site visitors are not licensed users.

Can I book rooms and equipment in Salesforce?

With Salesforce Scheduler, rooms and equipment are asset resources, which need Asset Scheduling, an extra-cost option. With MultiDatePick, the Booking component books any record as the resource, such as a room, vehicle, piece of equipment or a User, with capacity and conflict checks.

What is a good Lightning Scheduler alternative for custom objects?

MultiDatePick, when you want a calendar that reads and writes your own objects rather than adopting the Scheduler data model. It picks many dates, times and bookings on one calendar, shows them in five views, and is set up in a Setup Wizard with no Apex.

Is the standard Salesforce calendar a booking tool?

No. The standard calendar shows Events and object records on a month or week view. It is a viewer, not an input control, and it cannot select nine dates and create nine records.
