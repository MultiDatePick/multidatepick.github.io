Frequently Asked Questions

Short answers to what admins ask before choosing MultiDatePick: trust, data, editions, setup and price.

Setting it up? Getting Started, the User Guide and Troubleshooting have the steps.

The product

Is MultiDatePick no-code?

Yes. You build the calendar in a guided Setup Wizard, drop it on a record page in Lightning App Builder, and paste the Config Name; it then saves every pick to your own records. Apex and Flow are optional, for teams that want them.

What is MultiDatePick?

Three native Salesforce Lightning Web Components: Dates, Dates & Times and Resource Booking. Users pick many dates at once, add time slots, and book rooms, people or equipment. Every selection saves to your own Salesforce records.

Where can I use it in Salesforce?

Screen Flows, Lightning Record Pages, App Pages, Home Pages and Experience Cloud sites. Configure it once and place it anywhere.

What's the difference between the three components?

Dates picks many dates, with ranges. Dates & Times adds time slots. Resource Booking adds resources, capacity and conflict checks. See the component picker.

Is it only a month calendar?

No. Every component has six views: Calendar, Timeline, Week, Utilization, Agenda and Map. All six read the same records and save the same way. See the six views.

Can different pages show different views of the same data?

Yes. The view is set per placement, so a recruiter's page can open on Week, a facilities page on Calendar and a wall display on Agenda. One install, one set of field mappings.

Can I schedule people rather than rooms?

Yes. Point the resource at User and Timeline becomes a roster, Week a shift grid and Utilization a team capacity dashboard. The Config Library has ready-made Team Capacity Planning and Staff Shift Roster setups.

Does it handle resource booking and capacity?

Yes. Resource Booking handles one or many resources, per-slot capacity with live 3/5 badges, and conflict checks. See capacity in the User Guide.

Can I block out dates or times?

Yes. Point the component at any closure object. Block whole days, specific hours, or one resource at a time. See blocking dates.

Can I require notice, or limit how far ahead people book?

Yes. Minimum Notice (Hours) stops anything that starts too soon, and Book Up To (Days Ahead) opens a rolling window that moves forward every day. Buffer Between Bookings keeps cleanup time free after each booking. All three are checked again at Save.

Can people add a booking to Outlook or Google Calendar?

Yes. Turn on Show Add to Calendar After Saving and every save offers a calendar file (.ics) with an event for each record just saved. It opens in Outlook, Google Calendar and Apple Calendar, and works for guests on public sites. Nothing leaves Salesforce until the person downloads it. More on Add to Calendar.

Can a calendar button run my own Flow?

Yes. Add a custom action button in the Setup Wizard. It runs one of your Flows, or opens a link built from the record’s fields, from a record’s hover card or Agenda row, on the records picked in Edit Mode, or on its own after a save, move or delete. A button can show only for certain statuses.

Can I edit a whole recurring series at once?

Yes. In Edit Mode, pick one date of a recurring series and choose just this one, this and later, or the whole series, then change the date or time, or delete them, in one action. Dates saved from one pattern share a Series Id field, so the series is exactly what was created together.

Can I schedule records that have no date yet?

Yes. Turn on the Unscheduled list and records with no date wait as cards beside the calendar. Drag one onto a day to schedule it, or drag a scheduled record back to the list to clear its date.

Can the calendar show records on a map?

Yes. The Map view pins each record by its own or its resource’s address, beside a list in date order, and the location picker lets people choose a branch on a map, each with its next opening. Both use Salesforce’s built-in map, so no Salesforce Maps license is needed.

Can a booking ask for more details before it saves?

Yes. The booking form pop-up asks for the fields you choose, such as name, email, meeting type and topic, before the booking saves. It works on Lightning pages and on public Experience Cloud pages for guests.

Can I add our logo and brand colors to the calendar?

Yes. Header branding puts your logo, your company name, a line under the title, a note and a banner photo in the calendar’s top band, in your own band and button colors. It shows on every view and on public booking pages. More on header branding.

Does it work on Experience Cloud sites, including public pages?

Yes. All three components are in Experience Builder, for member sites and public guest booking pages. On a public page, set Show Other People's Bookings As to Busy for site guests, so visitors never see other people's names. See the guest page checklist.

Trust & security

Has it passed the Salesforce Security Review?

Yes. MultiDatePick passed the Salesforce AppExchange Security Review. Details are on Trust & Security.

Is my data stored outside Salesforce?

No. MultiDatePick is 100% native. Your data stays in your Salesforce org, with no external servers and no callouts.

Does it respect my sharing and field security?

Yes. Every read and save runs in the user's own permissions, so object access, field-level security and sharing apply to each record.

Is it accessible?

Yes. It works with a keyboard and with screen readers, and axe-core scans find zero violations. The VPAT 2.5 rates every applicable WCAG 2.1 A and AA criterion as Supports except two, rated Partially Supports: contrast, because admins can pick their own status colors, and reflow, because the month grid keeps seven columns at 320 pixels wide. The full report is on the accessibility page.

What languages does it support?

Nine: English, Spanish, French, German, Italian, Portuguese (Brazil), Japanese, Chinese (Simplified) and Hindi. It follows each user's Salesforce language. If your org uses the Translation Workbench, that language must be active in Setup, Translation Workbench, Translation Language Settings for MultiDatePick's translated picklist values, such as the status values, to show. Field labels and the calendar's own text are translated either way.

Setup & ownership

Do I need Apex or a developer?

No. Admins set everything up with clicks: the Setup Wizard, Lightning App Builder and Flow Builder. Start from 40+ ready-made configs in the Config Library, and learn inside your org with the bundled guided flows: Intro to MultiDatePick and a Deep Dive for each component.

Which permission sets do I assign?

MultiDatePick - User for everyone who uses the calendars. MultiDatePick - Setup for the admins who build configs; it includes the Setup Wizard. MultiDatePick - All Access for admins who do both. MultiDatePick - Guest for the guest user of a public Experience Cloud site. Users also need access to your own objects and fields.

How long does setup take?

Minutes for a standard use case. Import a config or run the Setup Wizard, then place the component. Getting Started walks through it.

Can you build a config for my use case?

Yes, free. If the Config Library has nothing close, describe your use case: who books what, your object and its fields, and the rules that matter. We build the config and email it to you to import in the Setup Wizard.

Is it a managed package?

Yes. It is a namespaced managed package, so it upgrades cleanly and never clashes with your own components. It will install from the Salesforce AppExchange at launch.

Does it work inside Salesforce Flows?

Yes. All three are Flow screen components. Set Config Name and every option comes from your saved config. A bundled Flow action turns the selection into a list you can loop over. See the Screen Flow Guide.

Pricing & availability

How much does MultiDatePick cost?

$20 per user per month, with every feature included. See pricing.

Is resource booking included, or is it an add-on?

Included. One plan with every feature: Dates, Dates & Times and Resource Booking. No tiers, no feature gates.

Is there a free trial?

Yes. A 30-day free trial, no card required.

When can I install it?

Get notified the day MultiDatePick is listed on the Salesforce AppExchange, and try every feature on the live demo today.

Which Salesforce editions are supported?

Any edition that supports Lightning Web Components and Screen Flows: Enterprise, Unlimited, Performance, Developer and Salesforce Platform, plus industry clouds such as Nonprofit, Education, Health, Financial Services, Public Sector and Manufacturing. Essentials and Professional are not supported because Salesforce restricts custom Lightning components there.
