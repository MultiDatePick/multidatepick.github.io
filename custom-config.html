Questions about the free config

Is the custom config really free?

Yes. There is no charge for the config and no obligation. MultiDatePick itself is $20 per user per month, with a 30-day free trial.

What do I get back?

A config file built for your use case, with its views, booking rules and statuses already set, and a short note on how to import it.

Do my field names have to match?

No. When you import the config, the Setup Wizard checks every field against your org and lets you pick your own field for any name that differs.

What should I leave out?

Customer records, personal data and passwords. Field names and settings are all we need.
