Salesforce class scheduling questions

Short answers to what people search.

Is there a class or course scheduling app for Salesforce?

Yes. MultiDatePick schedules classes and courses on your own Salesforce objects. Each session is a time slot on a training room or course with a seat limit, every slot shows the seats taken, such as 5/12, a full session cannot be booked, and learners can enroll themselves on an Experience Cloud site. No code.

How do I set a seat limit for each class session?

Add a number field such as Seats to the training room or course record and choose it as the Capacity Field in the Setup Wizard. Every session then shows a live badge such as 9/12, and the last seat closes the slot. A cancelled enrollment frees its seat.

Can a learner enroll in every session of a course at once?

Yes. Set Conflict Behavior to Skip. The learner picks all the sessions in one go, the open ones are booked, and any full session is skipped and listed, so nobody has to deselect it by hand.

Can students book a class on a public website?

Yes. Put the calendar on an Experience Cloud page and guests pick a session without logging in. The booking form pop-up asks for their name, email and anything else you need, and each enrollment saves as a record in your org.

Can one class run in several rooms at once?

Yes. With several resources and Capacity Aggregation set to Distinct, one session can book a main room and its overflow rooms together and count as one booking. The Multi-Site Workshop config in the Config Library is set up this way.

Can I schedule a course that repeats every week?

Yes. A recurring pattern books every Tuesday and Thursday for six weeks in one step, and series editing later moves or cancels this one session, this and later, or the whole course together.
