Shift capacity, for slots that need several people

Healthcare, warehousing, events and hospitality all book shifts that take more than one person.

How a shift fills
  • Capacity Field on the job or shift: openings per day
  • Every slot shows a live booked of capacity badge
  • The slot closes when it is full
  • Business hours per role, so nights and days differ
  • Status colors: Submitted, Confirmed, Worked
  • Edit inline to reassign or cancel

Your objects, your reports. Records land on the object you choose, in the record shape you choose: one per date, one per date range, one per time block, or a parent with sub-blocks. Nothing leaves your org.

The view that matters here is Timeline

Staffing is a who question before a when question. Timeline puts people down the side and two weeks across, so an over-committed week shows at a glance.

Point the resource at User and it becomes a roster. Timeline turns into a staff roster, Week into a shift grid, Utilization into a team capacity dashboard. Each person's working hours come from their own User record.

Utilization is read only, so pair it with Timeline or Week as the default view. More: the utilization heatmap.

Staffing and recruiting calendar questions

Short answers to what people search.

How do I track candidate availability in Salesforce?

Put the MultiDatePick Date component on the candidate record. A recruiter, or the candidate on an Experience Cloud site, clicks every day they can work, including scattered days and recurring patterns, and each date saves as a record on your own object. Set End Date Field to save a run of days as one range.

Can Salesforce schedule staff shifts?

Yes, with the MultiDatePick Booking component. Set the resource to User and each person becomes a bookable row. Timeline shows a two-week roster, Week places shifts at an exact hour, and Conflict Behavior set to Block stops anyone being booked twice.

Can one shift need more than one person?

Yes. Add a Capacity Field to the job or shift. Each slot shows a live booked of capacity badge and stays open until it is full.

How is this different from Salesforce Scheduler?

Salesforce Scheduler is a paid add-on built on its own data model for appointments. MultiDatePick books on your own objects, including User, with multi-date selection and shift capacity. See the full comparison of Salesforce Lightning Scheduler and MultiDatePick.
