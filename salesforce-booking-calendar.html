Capacity and conflict detection: no double booking

Every save is checked against existing bookings. You choose what happens on a clash.

BlockDefault. Taken slots cannot be clicked, so a double booking never starts.
SkipBook 20 dates at once; the open ones save and a banner names the ones skipped.
AllowOverlap is fine, for resources that can be shared freely.
SettingWhat it does
Capacity FieldA number on the resource. Slots stay bookable until full, with live 3/10 badges
Capacity AggregationCombined sums several resources (4 rooms of 5 = 20 seats). Distinct counts one event across rooms once; pair it with Block so the event saves only when every room is free
Hide Bookings With StatusCancelled bookings vanish from the grid and free their capacity
Conflict Look-Ahead DaysHow far ahead badges and conflicts are checked. Default 360
Buffer Between BookingsCleanup or turnaround minutes kept free after every booking, per resource. The slot grid, capacity badges, Week drag and the save check all honor it
Book Up To (Days Ahead)A rolling window: bookings open today plus N days out, moving forward every day
Minimum Notice (Hours)No booking that starts sooner than N hours from now, checked again at Save

Salesforce booking calendar questions

Short answers to what people search.

Does Salesforce have a booking calendar?

Not one that books your own objects. The standard Salesforce calendar shows Events and records, but it is a viewer, not a booking control. Salesforce Scheduler is a paid add-on built on its own data model. MultiDatePick Booking adds a booking calendar to any object, with time slots, capacity and conflict checks.

How do I book rooms or equipment in Salesforce?

Keep your rooms or equipment as records on any object, then point the MultiDatePick Booking component at that object in the Setup Wizard. Users pick a room, pick dates, click open time slots and book. Each booking saves as a record on your booking object, linked to the room and the parent record.

Can customers book on an Experience Cloud site?

Yes. Drop the Booking component on an Experience Cloud page, set Config Name and Static Parent Record Id, and visitors book open slots themselves. Guest users can read and create, so cancellations and edits belong behind a login.

How do I prevent double booking in Salesforce?

Set Conflict Behavior on the Booking component. Block makes taken slots unclickable, Skip saves the open dates of a multi-date booking and names the ones it skipped, and Allow permits overlap. With a Capacity Field, a slot stays open until it is full, shown as live badges like 3/10.

Can I see a calendar for every room or resource in Salesforce?

Yes. The Timeline view puts every room, vehicle or person down the side and dates across the top, with a combined capacity strip. The Utilization view shows how full each resource is per day as a heatmap, and the Week view shows open hours.

Can one booking reserve several resources at once?

Yes. Turn on Allow Booking Multiple Resources and users tick several rooms, people or vehicles. Capacity Aggregation decides how availability adds up: Combined for independent inventory, Distinct for one event that needs every resource free.

Is there a Lightning Web Component for booking in Salesforce?

Yes. MultiDatePick Booking is a Lightning Web Component for Screen Flows, Record Pages, App Pages, Home Pages and Experience Cloud pages. It is configured in a Setup Wizard, with no Apex.

How much does the MultiDatePick booking calendar cost?

$20 per user per month for every component and feature, with a 30-day free trial and no card required.
