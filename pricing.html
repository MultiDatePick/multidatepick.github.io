Pricing questions

Is every feature included?

Yes. The components do not check tiers, so what you install is the whole product, Gantt and site booking included.

Is there a free trial?

Yes: 30 days, no card, the complete product. The configurations you build stay when you buy.

Who needs a license?

Only the people who use the calendar. If twelve schedulers use it and four hundred people are simply the subject of the bookings, you license twelve.

How is it billed?

Through AppExchange Checkout, on Salesforce's own billing. You subscribe on the Salesforce AppExchange, with no separate procurement process.

Which editions work?

Enterprise, Unlimited, Performance, Developer and Salesforce Platform, plus industry clouds such as Nonprofit, Education, Health, Financial Services, Public Sector and Manufacturing. Essentials and Professional are not supported because Salesforce restricts custom Lightning components there.

Will you set it up for us?

The Setup Wizard takes minutes, with no code. If your case is not in the Config Library, describe it and we build the config for free.

Questions about pricing?

Procurement paperwork, security review, editions, or anything else: just ask.
