Common questions

Implementation project?

No. Admins set it up in the Setup Wizard, no code.

How is it billed?

Through AppExchange Checkout, on Salesforce's own billing. No separate contract with us.

Are there feature gates?

No. The components do not check tiers. What you install is the whole product.

Which editions work?

Enterprise, Unlimited, Performance, Developer and Salesforce Platform, plus industry clouds such as Nonprofit, Education, Health, Financial Services, Public Sector and Manufacturing. Essentials and Professional are not supported because Salesforce restricts custom Lightning components there.

When can we buy it?

Get the launch note the day MultiDatePick is listed on the Salesforce AppExchange.

Questions about pricing?

Procurement paperwork, security review, editions, or anything else: just ask.
