Salesforce was built to store a date, not to schedule

Out of the box, your entire date toolbox is two things: a Date field that holds one day, and a pair of Date fields that hold a single start and end range. That is it. The moment a user needs anything a real calendar does, it falls apart.

"I need every Monday and Thursday for the next six weeks." (that is not a range)
"Book the conference room these five scattered afternoons." (times plus a shared resource)
"Reserve one of our four vans, and don't let two crews grab the same one." (capacity plus conflicts)
"Three people are out on overlapping but different days." (many people, many date sets)

So admins bolt on workarounds: a child object with a manual "add another" button, a junction record typed in one date at a time, or a custom Lightning Web Component that a developer has to build and then maintain forever. It is slow to set up, clumsy to use, and fragile to own.
