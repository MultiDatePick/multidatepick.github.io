Partner questions

How much does a partner earn?

25% of every payment your client makes, for as long as the client stays. MultiDatePick is $25 per user per month, so that is $6.25 per user per month. A client with 50 users pays $1,250 a month, and your share is $312.50 a month.

When is the partner share paid?

Quarterly, after the client pays.

When do I register a client?

Before or when the client starts with MultiDatePick, using the registration form on this page.

Does my client pay more because a partner is involved?

No. Your client pays the same $25 per user per month as everyone else, with the same 30-day free trial.

Can my team use MultiDatePick in our own org and demo orgs?

Yes. Partners get free licenses for their team and demo orgs.

Do I need to write code to deliver it?

No. MultiDatePick is set up in a guided Setup Wizard with 150+ settings, on any standard or custom object. You can start from one of 47 ready-made configs in the Config Library and map it to your client's fields.
