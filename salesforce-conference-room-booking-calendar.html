Salesforce room booking questions

Short answers to what people search.

Can you book conference rooms in Salesforce?

Not out of the box. Standard Salesforce has no room booking control. MultiDatePick Booking adds one: keep your rooms as records on any object, and people pick a room, pick one or many dates, click open time slots and book. Each booking saves as a record on your own booking object.

How do I stop two people booking the same meeting room?

Set Conflict Behavior to Block. Taken slots are drawn as taken and cannot be clicked, and every save is checked again against existing bookings, so a clash never saves.

Can I do desk booking or desk hoteling in Salesforce?

Yes. Make each desk zone a resource with a Capacity Field and turn on Show Availability Count, so people see how many desks are left until the zone is full. Disable Time Slot Grid (Full-Day Mode) books desks for the whole day. The Desk Hoteling config in the Config Library is a ready starting point.

Can I see every room's schedule on one screen?

Yes. The Timeline view puts every room down the side and dates across the top, with multi-day holds drawn as one bar. The Utilization view shows how full each room is per day as a heatmap, and the Week view shows the day by hour.

Can one booking reserve several rooms at once?

Yes. Turn on Allow Booking Multiple Resources and people tick several rooms for the same slot in one action. Capacity Aggregation decides how capacity adds up across the rooms.

Can each room have its own hours and closures?

Yes. Business Hours Start Field and Business Hours End Field read each room's hours from the room record. Blocked dates come from your own closure records, for a whole day or part of a day, and for every room or just one.

Where can people book a room from?

Anywhere you place the Booking component: a record page, an App or Home page, a Screen Flow, or an Experience Cloud site page. On App, Home and Experience pages, set Config Name and Static Parent Record Id.
